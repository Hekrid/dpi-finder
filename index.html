DPI Finder

A short aim test that plays at several hidden sensitivities, measures how your hand actually moves, and recommends the DPI and sensitivity you aim best with.

How it works, and tips for a clean result
  • You'll play a warm-up and then several rounds. Each round silently changes your sensitivity, so you can't bias the result.
  • Each round has flicks (click each orange target as fast as you can) and tracking (keep the crosshair on a strafing target).
  • We look at speed, accuracy, overshoot, undershoot, correction movements and tracking lag at each sensitivity, then find the peak.
  • Play in a full-screen window, sit how you normally game, and turn off mouse acceleration ("Enhance pointer precision" on Windows).
  • Press Esc anytime to pause.
